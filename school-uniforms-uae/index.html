Working with schools and distributors

We supply schools directly and also act as a manufacturing partner for uniform resellers. Fabric is wash-tested and colourfast so uniforms survive a full academic year.

Built for daily washing and growth spurts

School uniforms live a hard life: daily wear, frequent hot washes, and children who outgrow sizes mid-year. We cut school polos, shirts and PE kits with reinforced seams and colour-fast fabrics, and we keep every school's size matrix and colour references on file. When a parent needs two more shirts in October, we can re-run them without re-approving the whole program.

How school rollouts work with us

Typical flow: we present fabric and colour options, produce a sample set across sizes for the school to approve, then manufacture to the size breakdown per grade. Deliveries are scheduled before term starts, and mid-year top-ups are turned around in days rather than weeks because patterns and digitised logos are already on file. Frequently asked questions

What is the lead time for a school uniform programme?

Samples in 5–7 days, bulk in 2–3 weeks per style. Re-orders on approved styles ship much faster because patterns and thread are already held.

Can you match our existing school colours?

Yes — we match Pantone references for fabric, thread and trims.
