About Allegiant Attire

A garment factory in New Industrial Area 2, Ajman — cutting, stitching and printing under one roof since 2016.

WhatsApp +971 58 204 5242 Build a quote

Allegiant Attire was built on a simple problem: UAE brands were waiting weeks for samples and months for bulk, because design, stitching and printing were split across different suppliers and different countries. We put all of it in one building in Ajman.

  • Dedicated lines for knits, wovens and headwear
  • 180–400 GSM certified fabrics, wash-tested
  • Same-day DTF, 5-day sampling, 2-week bulk
  • Factory-direct pricing from 12 pcs to 50,000 pcs

What we do

Blank and custom apparel for men, women and kids — t-shirts, polos, hoodies, activewear, caps, school uniforms, corporate uniforms and safety wear — plus six in-house decoration methods: DTF, screen printing, sublimation, embroidery, vinyl and heat transfer, and custom labelling.

How we work

Cut, stitch, print, press, pack. Quality control runs at every stage rather than at the end, which is how we hold re-order consistency across bulk runs.

Where we deliver

Same-day DTF in Dubai, 1–2 days across the UAE, pressed, poly-bagged and size-sorted on arrival.

Inside the Ajman facility

Everything we sell is made or finished under one roof in New Industrial Area 2, Ajman: cutting through stitching, DTF printing, screen printing, sublimation and embroidery, then finishing — pressing, size-sorting and poly-bagging. Keeping the whole chain in-house is why we can quote quickly, sample quickly and hit same-day windows in Dubai when an event simply cannot move.

How we work with clients

Most of our business runs on WhatsApp: you send artwork and quantities, we reply with a digital mockup and per-piece pricing, usually the same day. First orders always include approval steps — mockup, then physical sample for larger runs. Bulk deliveries are counted, checked and size-sorted before dispatch, and every file stays on record for fast reorders.
