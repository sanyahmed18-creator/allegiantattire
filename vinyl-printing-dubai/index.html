A focused finish for names, numbers and simple marks

Vinyl and heat transfer are useful when an order needs clear, individual names or numbers, or a single-colour mark applied to a garment. They are a practical choice for team kits and staff personalisation where each piece may need a different name or number. Share the garment, artwork and quantity with us so we can check that the design and fabric are suitable before confirming a quote.

How to request a vinyl printing quote

  1. Tell us the garment type, fabric if known, quantity and size breakdown.
  2. Send the artwork, mark colour, print position and any names or numbers that vary by piece.
  3. Share your target delivery date and where the order needs to go.
  4. Review the written quote and any mockup or sample details before approving production.

A website enquiry opens a WhatsApp draft; it is not sent until you press Send. Final method, artwork placement, pricing and timing are confirmed with you directly.

Choosing a decoration method

If your artwork is full colour or contains gradients, compare vinyl with DTF printing. For larger runs with a limited number of colours, ask about screen printing. If a stitched finish is a better match, see our embroidery service. The right method depends on the garment, fabric, design, quantity and intended use, so ask us to confirm suitability before you order.
