Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

DTF and print services start at 1 piece. Custom blanks start at 10–12 pieces per style. Cut-and-sew factory runs start at 100 pieces per colour.

How fast can you deliver custom clothing in Dubai?

In-house DTF is produced in Ajman for same-day Dubai orders, subject to stock and artwork approval. Standard custom orders are delivered in 1–2 days across the UAE, pressed, poly-bagged and size-sorted.

Do you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sampling takes 5–7 days; bulk production runs 2–3 weeks depending on quantity and print method.

Which print method should I choose?

DTF for full-colour, low-quantity and urgent jobs; screen printing for bulk runs above 100 pieces with Pantone-matched inks; sublimation for all-over activewear; embroidery for polos, caps and uniforms.

Can you handle labelling and packaging?

Yes — woven neck labels, hang tags, size chips and branded packaging are available to make a blank garment your own brand.
