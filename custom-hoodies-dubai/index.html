Fabric and fit options

Choose between a classic retail fit and an oversized streetwear block. Fleece weight is the single biggest driver of hand feel and price — 300 GSM for a light layer, 400 GSM for a winter-weight hoodie.

Fleece weights that make sense in the UAE

Hoodies in the UAE sell hardest from November to February and inside over-air-conditioned offices and cinemas year-round. French terry works for mild weather and indoor use; brushed fleece suits outdoor winter events and staff uniforms for cold storage and night shifts. We stock both and will tell you honestly when a zip-up is more practical than a pullover for your use case.

Decorating hoodies without cracking prints

Fleece is thick and textured, so print method matters. DTF film bonds cleanly to fleece and flexes without cracking, making it the default for full-colour designs. Embroidery gives crests and logos a premium, permanent finish on the chest. For large back prints on bulk orders, screen printing keeps the per-piece cost down while staying wash-fast. We test-press on the actual fabric before the run.
