When to choose screen printing

If you are ordering more than 100 pieces with a 1–4 colour logo, screen printing beats DTF on price and on wash durability. Below 100 pieces, DTF is usually cheaper because there is no screen setup.

Why bulk runs still favour screen printing

Once the screens are made, ink cost per piece drops sharply — that is why runs above roughly 100 pieces with simple artwork almost always go screen. Colours are Pantone-matched, and a properly cured screen print survives 50 or more industrial washes without fading. For uniforms and merch that must look identical across hundreds of pieces and many months, screen printing remains the benchmark.

Artwork requirements for sharp screens

Vector files (AI, EPS or PDF) give the cleanest separations; we can also work from high-resolution PNGs and re-draw where needed. Fine lines below roughly half a millimetre and tiny reversed-out text are the usual troublemakers — our team flags these in the mockup stage and suggests adjustments before screens are burned. A test print on the real garment fabric is part of every approval.

Frequently asked questions

How many colours can you screen print?

Up to 6 spot colours per placement. Gradients and photographic artwork move to simulated process or DTF.
