DTF vs screen printing

DTF wins below 100 pieces and for detailed, multi-colour artwork. Screen printing wins above 100 pieces on 1–3 spot colours, where per-unit cost drops and Pantone accuracy matters.

Artwork requirements

PNG with a transparent background at 300 DPI, or a vector file. We can prepare low-resolution logos for a small setup fee.

What DTF is good at — and when it is not

DTF (direct-to-film) handles unlimited colours, gradients and photo-quality artwork with no minimum quantity, and it works on cotton, polyester and blends. That makes it the right tool for launches, influencer merch, staff packs and urgent event shirts. Where it is not the right tool: very large runs of simple one- or two-colour logos, where screen printing is cheaper per piece, and all-over prints, where sublimation wins.
