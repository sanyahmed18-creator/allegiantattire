Embroidery vs printing on polos

Embroidery outlasts printing on a polo and reads as premium on a uniform — it is what hotels and corporate clients expect. DTF or screen print is faster and cheaper for short-run event polos with detailed artwork.

Sampling

We make a physical sample with your logo in 5–7 days so procurement can sign off fabric, fit and thread colour before bulk.

Pique, jersey or performance knit?

Cotton pique is the classic office and retail polo — structured collar, breathable, takes embroidery beautifully. Jersey knits feel softer and suit hospitality crews. Performance knits wick sweat for outdoor teams, security staff and event crews working the UAE heat. We will recommend the knit based on where your team actually wears it, and send fabric photos before you commit.

Branding placements that look premium

Left-chest embroidery remains the most requested placement for corporate polos — small, sharp and durable. Sleeve prints, back-neck labels and contrast tipping on collars add brand detail without shouting. For events where the logo must be seen from a distance, a large DTF back print works better than embroidery. We mock up every placement option before production so you can compare.
