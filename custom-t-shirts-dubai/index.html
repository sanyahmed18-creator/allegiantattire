Which print method fits your t-shirt order?

DTF is the fastest and handles full-colour artwork with no minimum — ideal for launches, onboarding packs and events. Screen printing is cheaper per unit above roughly 100 pieces and survives 50+ washes with Pantone-matched inks. Sublimation is for polyester activewear where you want the colour inside the fibre rather than on top of it.

Turnaround times

Samples in 5–7 days, bulk production in 2–3 weeks. Rush orders using existing blanks and in-house DTF can be pressed and delivered the same day inside Dubai.

Who we make t-shirts for

Fabrics and fits we run every week

Most Dubai orders run on 180 GSM combed cotton for events and merch, and 220-240 GSM for retail-quality drops and oversized fits. We also keep poly-cotton blends for workwear crews who wash garments hard and often. Tell us how the shirt will be used — daily uniform, one-day event, or a brand drop — and we will point you at the fabric that survives it, not just the cheapest one.

How ordering works

Send your artwork as AI, PDF or high-resolution PNG over WhatsApp or email. We come back with a digital mockup and a per-piece price, usually the same day. Once you approve, we press a physical sample for larger runs, then move to bulk production, QC, size-sorting and delivery. You approve the sample before the full run starts, so there are no surprises at delivery.
