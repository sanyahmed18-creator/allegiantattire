Digitising and approval

Every logo is digitised before production. You approve a stitched sample so thread colour, density and placement are locked before bulk runs.

Digitising your logo

Embroidery starts with digitising — converting your logo into a stitch file. This is a one-time setup per design, and the stitch count it produces drives the per-piece price: dense, large designs cost more than compact chest logos. We keep your digitised file on record, so repeat orders of the same logo go straight to production with no setup delay.

Where embroidery beats printing

Polos, caps, aprons and corporate uniforms all benefit from embroidery's raised, tactile finish — it reads as premium in a way flat prints cannot. It also survives hot industrial washing better than any ink, which matters for hospitality and healthcare uniforms. For big, full-colour artwork we will usually steer you to DTF on the back and embroidery on the chest — the combination most uniform programs settle on.
