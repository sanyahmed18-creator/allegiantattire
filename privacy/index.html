Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · How this website handles information when you browse, request a quote or contact Allegiant Attire.

Who we are

This website is operated under the name Allegiant Attire, a clothing manufacturer based in New Industrial Area 2, Ajman, United Arab Emirates. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at info@allegiantattire.store or call +971 58 204 5242. This website does not provide customer accounts.

Information you choose to share

When you request a quote or order, the website prepares a message for WhatsApp. The draft can contain your name, phone number, company, garment and quantity details, delivery emirate or address, preferred payment method, artwork notes and other information you enter. A quote form can also prepare an email draft for a price-list request. The website does not send those drafts to Allegiant Attire by itself: you must open the relevant app and press Send. Until then, no request has been submitted to us.

If you call, email or message us, we receive the details and files you include. We use them to answer your question, prepare a quote, discuss or fulfil an order, arrange delivery and provide customer support. Please do not include payment-card numbers, government ID numbers, health information or other sensitive details in a WhatsApp or email message.

WhatsApp, email and other third parties

Choosing an external contact link opens WhatsApp or your email application. A WhatsApp draft is carried in the link to WhatsApp so it can be prefilled; WhatsApp may process that information even if you do not press Send. A mail draft is handed to your device's email application. Once you send a message, the receiving service and its provider handle it under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not control those services.

If you place an order, relevant details may be used by the Allegiant Attire team and service providers involved in quoting, production, customer support or delivery. Website infrastructure providers also process technical data needed to serve pages. The exact providers and their processing locations can change; third-party services may process information outside the UAE. Review the provider notices before using their services.

Analytics and technical data

Some marketing pages load Google Analytics (measurement ID G-6607LM6K22) and Cloudflare Insights. Those providers may receive technical and usage information such as the page requested, browser and device details, referral information, approximate location and network identifiers. Depending on the provider and your browser, cookies or similar technologies may be used. The Privacy Policy page itself does not load those analytics scripts. We do not operate a server-side analytics or advertising profile in this static website.

For details about provider handling, see Google's Privacy Policy and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Browser privacy settings, content blockers and cookie controls can limit analytics, though site features or external links may behave differently.

Browser storage

The site uses your browser's local storage for site-operation settings and design tools. Depending on the feature used, it can hold catalog or estimate settings, display preferences, and Design Lab mockup information such as text, layer positions or artwork images. This data stays in the browser profile on that device; it is not automatically uploaded to an Allegiant Attire server or synchronized across devices. Shopping bag and wishlist state are temporary page state, not a customer account.

Local data may remain after you leave the site until you clear the website's storage in your browser settings. Clearing it can reset saved design work or site settings. Do not use a shared device for private artwork, and do not treat the client-side admin/editor tools as secure storage or authentication.

Purpose, retention and security

We use information to respond to requests, prepare and manage clothing orders, operate and improve the website, protect the site and meet legal obligations. We keep messages and order-related records for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and any applicable record-keeping requirements. Browser-stored information remains until you or your browser remove it. Analytics providers apply their own retention settings.

We use reasonable measures for a small static website, but no website, email or messaging service can be guaranteed completely secure. Contact channels and analytics are operated by third parties, so avoid sending information that is not needed for your enquiry or order.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to send a prepared message, manage cookies and site storage through your browser, or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp. Where applicable privacy law grants you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, you can make a request using the contact details above. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

This notice is intended to describe the current website behavior and may be updated when the site or its providers change. The version date appears at the top of this page. If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first so we can review it.
