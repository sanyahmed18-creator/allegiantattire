Team kits

Send us a design or a club crest and we will produce a full kit with player names and numbers sublimated in. Samples in 5–7 days, bulk in 2–3 weeks.

Performance fabrics and sublimation

Activewear runs on moisture-wicking polyester knits, and the natural partner for it is sublimation — the dye bonds inside the fibre, so all-over prints never crack, peel or fade no matter how hard the garment is used. That makes it the standard for gym wear, team kits, cycling jerseys and event activewear with full-coverage designs.

Private-label activewear from 100 pieces

For fitness brands we run cut-and-sew activewear from 100 pieces per colour: your patterns or ours, your woven labels, hang tags and packaging, so garments arrive shelf-ready under your own brand. Samples take 5-7 days and bulk runs 2-3 weeks. Start with one style, prove the sell-through, then extend the range on repeat production cycles.
