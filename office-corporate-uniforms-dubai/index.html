From reception to warehouse — one supplier

Most companies need more than one garment type: embroidered polos or shirts for office staff, tees for events, caps and hi-vis for site and warehouse teams. Running all of it through one factory keeps colours, logos and quality consistent across every garment, and gives you one invoice and one point of contact instead of three vendors.

Rolling out uniforms to a growing team

We keep your size matrix, approved samples and digitised logos on file. When new joiners start, you order only what you need — there is no need to re-run the whole program. Per-employee packing can be arranged so HR hands out ready sets, and repeat orders typically move faster than first runs because nothing has to be re-approved.
