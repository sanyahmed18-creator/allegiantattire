Private Label Clothing Manufacturing in the UAE

We manufacture private label apparel for UAE and regional brands: streetwear, athleisure, kidswear and workwear. Everything from pattern-making to finished, labelled, poly-bagged goods happens in one Ajman facility, so you are not chasing three suppliers across two countries.

  • Pattern making, grading and tech pack development
  • Fabric sourcing: 180–400 GSM knits, wovens and fleece

  • Factory-direct tiers from 12 pcs up to 50,000 pcs

The private label process

Send a tech pack, a reference garment or even a sketch. We quote fabric and construction, sample it, you approve, we run bulk and deliver size-sorted and labelled.

Branding and packaging

Woven labels, printed size chips, hang tags, polybags with your logo and custom mailer boxes — the finishing that separates a private label from a printed blank.

Your brand, our factory

Private label means the garment arrives as your product: your woven neck labels, hang tags, size chips and branded packaging, all produced and applied in-house. You can start from our proven blank blocks — tees, polos, hoodies, caps — or bring a tech pack for full cut-and-sew. Either way, nothing leaves the factory with our name on it.

From 100 pieces to repeat production

Cut-and-sew runs start at 100 pieces per colour, with sampling in 5-7 days and bulk in 2-3 weeks. Patterns, graded sizes and label artwork stay on file, which makes restocks fast — most repeat orders ship without any re-approval cycle. Many UAE brands start with a single tee style and scale into multi-style ranges this way.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me to start a private label run?

A tech pack, a reference garment, or sketches with measurements. We can develop the tech pack for you.

What is the bulk MOQ for private label?

100 pieces per colour per style for cut-and-sew; 12 pieces for branded blanks.

Do you handle fabric sourcing?

Yes — we source certified knits, wovens and fleece in 180–400 GSM weights.
