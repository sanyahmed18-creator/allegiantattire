DEPLOY GUIDE — ALLEGIANT ATTIRE SEO UPDATE
==========================================
Reviewed 6 October 2026. The local repository changes do not publish themselves.

IMPORTANT: Live fetches on the review date still showed the older React
storefront, generated product ratings/review counts and unsupported promotional
metrics. The live sitemap also omitted /blog/, and the live robots.txt comments
conflicted with its actual crawler rules. These are corrected in the repository,
but only a deployment (and the correct Cloudflare/host configuration) will
replace the production responses.

BEFORE DEPLOYING
1. Ask the business owner to verify the exact address/map pin, phone, public
   inbox, hours, founding year, services, service areas, prices, MOQs, fabric
   specifications, wash tests and all delivery/sample/production claims.
2. Confirm whether the current robots.txt model-data/training crawler blocks
   are the intended owner policy. Search crawlers and training/data-use bots
   are separate choices; a CDN/WAF can still block a bot.
3. From this repository root, run:

     ./apply-all.sh .

   It patches the shipped JS, regenerates the blog/sitemap and validates the
   static HTML. Expected result: 0 errors and 1 intentional screen-reader-only
   homepage-heading warning. Also run:

     node --check assets/index-CAFUB5Vv.js
     node --check custom.js

DEPLOY
Publish the complete current site document root with your existing hosting
workflow: root index.html, 404.html, service/about/contact/blog route folders,
assets, custom.css/custom.js, images, videos, icons, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
Keep the host’s expected custom-domain/CNAME setup. Do not publish .git; helper
scripts and audit notes are not runtime assets. .htaccess only works on a
compatible Apache host; GitHub Pages/static hosts need equivalent edge settings.

AFTER DEPLOY
- Check the visible homepage, a service page and all five blog URLs.
- Check /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml at the live domain.
- Confirm no generated ratings/testimonials or unverified summary metrics appear.
- Verify HTTPS/canonical redirects, Cloudflare WAF/CSP/cache, and email display.
- Verify the site in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap; this is not
  done by the repository changes. Confirm the Google Business Profile separately.

These changes improve crawlability and clarity; they do not guarantee rankings,
traffic, indexing, or AI citations. See AUDIT-REPORT.md for detail and official
Google/OpenAI/Anthropic/Perplexity crawler references.
