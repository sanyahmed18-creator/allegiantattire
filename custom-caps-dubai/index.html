3D puff vs flat embroidery

3D puff gives a raised, tactile logo that reads well from a distance — the standard for streetwear and team caps. Flat embroidery is cleaner for small corporate logos.

Styles we brand every week

The most requested are classic 5-panel and 6-panel cotton caps, truckers with mesh backs for outdoor crews, and snapbacks for merch drops. Colours can be matched to your brand palette, and closures — snap, buckle or velcro — chosen per style. Send us a photo of any cap you like and we will match the construction before quoting.

Embroidery vs DTF on caps

Raised front-crest embroidery is the classic cap finish and the right choice for logos worn daily — it survives sweat, sun and repeated handling. DTF works better for photographic or many-colour artwork, small runs and event giveaways where embroidery setup would dominate the cost. We will show you both finishes on your actual artwork before you decide.
