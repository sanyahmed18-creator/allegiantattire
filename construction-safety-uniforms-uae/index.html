Compliance and specification

We build to your safety specification and reflective-tape layout. Share the standard you must meet and we will confirm the construction before sampling.

Hi-vis and workwear that holds up on site

Site crews in the UAE need breathable fabrics that survive dust, sun and industrial laundry. We manufacture and brand safety vests, work polos, coveralls and caps with reflective tape placement suited to your specification, and we can work to the spec sheet attached to your tender. Logos are applied in embroidery or print methods chosen to survive site washing — we will advise which for each garment.

Ordering for a project workforce

Projects rarely need one size run: tell us the crew breakdown and we manufacture mixed-size batches, packed and labelled per site or subcontractor. When the workforce scales up mid-project, repeat batches of the same spec move quickly because patterns and artwork stay on file. Deliveries can be scheduled to site offices across Dubai, Sharjah and the northern emirates.
